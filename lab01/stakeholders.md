# Stakeholder Analysis and Engagement Priorities

## Priority Stakeholders
The Project Manager must give immediate attention to **S01 (Head of the IT Department)** and **S02 (Supervising Lecturer / Project Manager)** because both possess high influence (H) and high interest (H) in the project outcome. S01 controls project funding, aligns the endeavor with departmental goals, and requires milestone validation at week 7 (MVP demo) and week 14 (pilot). S02 governs academic alignment, manages team execution, and requires fast decision-making loops to keep the 14-week schedule on track. Managing these two key stakeholders ensures scope boundaries, budget allocations, and strategic direction remain stable throughout the project lifecycle.

## Veto Stakeholder
**S09 (Data Protection Officer / Registrar)** serves as the primary "veto" stakeholder for this project. Although S09 exhibits low day-to-day interest (L), they hold high influence (H) with regulatory authority to block or shut down the week 14 pilot if student data handling, authentication privacy, or grade storage fail to meet institutional data protection standards.
